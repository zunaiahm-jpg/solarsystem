The solar system is full of extremes, surprises, and numbers so huge they're hard to picture. Here are some of our favorites, organized by theme and linked to the pages where you can learn more.

  • 01

    Hottest planet: Venus at 464 °C — hotter than Mercury despite being farther from the Sun.

    Learn more about Venus →
  • 02

    Coldest planet: Uranus at −224 °C, even though Neptune is farther away.

    Learn more about Uranus →
  • 03

    Tallest volcano: Olympus Mons on Mars — 2.5× the height of Everest.

    Learn more about Mars →
  • 04

    Largest moon: Ganymede (Jupiter) — bigger than the planet Mercury.

    Learn more about Jupiter →
  • 05

    Shortest day: Jupiter at just 9.9 hours.

    Learn more about Jupiter →
  • 06

    Longest year: Neptune at 165 Earth years per orbit.

    Learn more about Neptune →
