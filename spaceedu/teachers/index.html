Lesson Plans

Grades 3–645–60 minutes

Scale of the Solar System

Students build a scale model of the solar system using everyday objects to understand the enormous distances between planets. This hands-on activity connects mathematics (ratios and proportions) with space science.

Grades 4–740–50 minutes

Why Is Mars Red?

Students investigate why Mars appears red by conducting a simple rust experiment with steel wool and vinegar. They connect the chemical process of oxidation to the iron-rich Martian surface.

View lesson plan →
Grades 2–530–45 minutes

Phases of the Moon

Students use a lamp and a ball to model the phases of the Moon, learning why we see different shapes throughout the month. They keep a Moon journal to track real observations over two weeks.
