Head to head

Jupiter vs Saturn

Jupiter and Saturn are the solar system's two gas giants, both made almost entirely of hydrogen and helium. Jupiter is the heavyweight — more massive than everything else in the solar system combined besides the Sun — while Saturn is famous for the most spectacular ring system of any planet and the largest number of confirmed moons.

The verdict

Jupiter wins on size, mass, and storm power; Saturn wins on rings and moon count. Together they shaped the architecture of the entire solar system, flinging comets and asteroids with their enormous gravity.
