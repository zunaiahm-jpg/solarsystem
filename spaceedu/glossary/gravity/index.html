Gravity

The force that pulls objects with mass toward each other. Gravity keeps planets orbiting the Sun, keeps the Moon orbiting Earth, and keeps your feet on the ground. The more massive an object, the stronger its gravity: the Sun's gravity holds the entire solar system together.
