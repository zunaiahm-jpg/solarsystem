Features

Built for immersive exploration

Every planet, moon, and star in Solaris is driven by real data, then rendered in adaptive 8K so you can wander the system at your own pace.

Real Orbital Motion

Planets and moons follow genuine orbital math. Distances and timescales are algorithmically compressed so the system stays lively instead of empty and slow.

Every major body is dressed in high-resolution NASA imagery, streamed at the right detail for your device so surfaces stay crisp without stutter.

  • 8K planetary surface maps
  • Earth day & night imagery

Live Earth Clouds

Earth is wrapped in daily real-world cloud imagery, so the planet you orbit reflects the weather patterns of the actual day.

  • Fresh daily cloud maps
  • Realistic atmosphere glow
  • Blended day/night terminator

Immersive Planetary Terrain

Fly close and the view drops into detailed planetary surfaces, letting you study each world up close rather than as a distant dot.

  • Surface-level camera framing

WebXR VR Support

Put on a headset and step inside the solar system. Solaris supports WebXR so you can stand among the planets in full stereoscopic 3D.

  • One-tap Enter VR
  • Room-scale scale model
  • Runs in the browser — no install

Deep-Sky Backdrop

A realistic Milky Way surrounds the scene, with toggleable stars, nebulae, and constellation lines to place every planet in its cosmic context.

  • 6K/8K star maps
  • Nebulae & constellation overlays
  • Searchable object index

The experience

About Solaris

Solaris is an interactive model of the solar system that is quite, but not entirely, realistic.

The vast distances and differences in space and time that are present in the real solar system can make observation boring or intimidating. Solaris contains real data and real orbital math; but distances and differences in space and time are algorithmically reduced to make the exploration experience more interesting and fun.

Key features

  • All major celestial objects with real characteristics and 8K NASA textures
  • A realistic Milky Way background and daily Earth cloud imagery
  • Focused, immersive exploration with WebXR VR support
