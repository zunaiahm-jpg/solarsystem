Boilerplate

Short (35 words)

Solaris is a free, browser-based 8K model of the solar system with real orbital data, live ISS, asteroid and space-weather feeds, narrated classroom tours and WebXR VR. No install, no account.

Long (90 words)

Solaris is an interactive model of the solar system that runs entirely in the browser. Every planet, moon and star is driven by real data and dressed in 8K NASA-derived imagery, while distances and time are deliberately compressed so the system stays visible and alive. Live layers track the International Space Station, this week’s near-Earth asteroid passes and the Sun’s current activity from NASA and NOAA feeds. Narrated tours aligned to NGSS standards, printable lesson plans, a low-bandwidth mode and WebXR support make it ready for any classroom or headset.

Changelog

Solaris ships continuously. Versions below group notable releases; dates are the day a release reached solarisvr.com.

  1. 2 Sep 2026

    1.4.0 — Live sky, narrated tours, classroom pages

    The biggest classroom release yet.

    • Live layers in the explorer: ISS ride-along with real position, altitude and speed; near-Earth asteroid overlay from NASA NeoWs; space-weather halo on the Sun driven by NOAA GOES X-ray class and Kp index. All off by default, toggled from the settings panel.
    • Narrated guided tours using the browser’s Web Speech API, with always-on captions, keyboard control and three NGSS-aligned routes (grades 3–5, 6–8, 9–12).
    • Low-bandwidth mode: ~5 MB texture set, 4K sky, no bloom; automatic on Data Saver.
    • New pages: Today in space dashboard, printable and embeddable lesson plans, About & data sources, this press page, and deep-link pages for every world.
    • Deep links: ?focus=, ?tour=, ?layers=, ?ride=iss, ?lite=1.
    • schema.org JSON-LD across the site, refreshed sitemap and robots.txt.
  2. 28 Aug 2026

    1.3.0 — Live data proxy

    • Same-origin /api/live endpoint aggregating ISS, asteroid and space-weather feeds with short caching, so classrooms never hit provider rate limits.
    • Settings panel restyled as a command palette with sections.
  3. 22 Aug 2026

    1.2.0 — SpaceEdu and space chat

    • SpaceEdu learning site added under /spaceedu/ with 16K image gallery.
    • Space chat assistant and refreshed header navigation on the landing page.
  4. 20 Aug 2026

    1.1.0 — Community

    • Registration and feedback forms backed by a Neon Postgres database with honeypot and timing anti-spam.
    • Thank-you and notification emails; admin dashboard for responses.
    • First sitemap published.
  5. 1 Aug 2026

    1.0.0 — Launch

    • Semi-realistic solar system with 8K NASA-derived textures, real orbital periods, tilts and inclinations.
    • Photographic all-sky plate, named stars, constellations and nebulae layers.
    • Daily live Earth clouds, immersive planetary surfaces, WebXR VR with comfort rig and wrist HUD.
    • Object panels with absolute and Earth-relative statistics, mission video and NASA news.
