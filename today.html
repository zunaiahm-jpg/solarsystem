Use it in class

Warm-up (5 min)

Project this page. Ask: is the ISS over land or ocean right now? Which hemisphere? Refresh after two minutes and estimate how far it moved.

Data literacy (15 min)

Sort the asteroid table by miss distance. Which pass is closest in lunar distances? Convert it to kilometres and compare to the Moon.

Extension

If Kp is 4 or more, look up tonight’s aurora forecast for your latitude. Link the Kp number to the space-weather halo in the Scale of Space lesson.
