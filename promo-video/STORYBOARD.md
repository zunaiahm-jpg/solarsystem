# SolarisVR Promo Ad Storyboard

**Runtime:** 36 seconds, 30 fps  
**Master:** 1920x1080 16:9  
**Alternate:** 1080x1920 9:16  
**Direction:** A cinematic, UI-adjacent journey from a dark, quiet starfield into a bright, explorable solar system. The visual language borrows the existing product palette: deep navy, electric cyan, solar orange, and cool white.

## Scene 1: Step inside | 00:00-00:06
- **On-screen:** `A NEW VIEW OF HOME` and `STEP INSIDE`
- **Voiceover/caption:** “What if you could step inside the solar system?”
- **Motion:** A glowing procedural sun ignites in the center while two orbit ellipses ease into place. Stars twinkle and the camera grid drifts slowly to establish depth.
- **Transition:** Crossfade into a wider orbital tableau.

## Scene 2: Explore every world | 00:06-00:16
- **On-screen:** `REAL ORBITAL MOTION`, `Explore every world. At your pace.`
- **Voiceover/caption:** “Explore every world, moving through real orbital motion at your pace.”
- **Motion:** Planetary spheres glide along different orbit paths with subtle parallax and scale changes. Rings and orbit lines create a living system rather than a static diagram.
- **Transition:** The blue planet scales forward as the scene crossfades.

## Scene 3: See the unseen | 00:14-00:24
- **On-screen:** `ADAPTIVE 8K / LIVE EARTH`, `See the unseen.`
- **Voiceover/caption:** “See the unseen in crisp detail, with high-resolution NASA textures and living clouds.”
- **Motion:** A ringed blue planet becomes the hero object. A cool atmospheric glow, tracking label, and slow push-in imply camera-level exploration.
- **Transition:** Orange accent wipe into the VR scene.

## Scene 4: Put on wonder | 00:22-00:32
- **On-screen:** `WEBXR / NO INSTALL`, `Put on wonder.`, `ENTER VR ↗`
- **Voiceover/caption:** “Then put on wonder. Solaris runs in your browser, ready for WebXR.”
- **Motion:** Small worlds orbit a warm sun while the CTA control and orange type lock to a lightweight HUD. The composition breathes with a slow camera drift.
- **Transition:** Fade down to the brand lockup.

## Scene 5: Call to action | 00:30-00:36
- **On-screen:** `SOLARISVR`, `THE SOLAR SYSTEM, YOURS TO EXPLORE`, `solarisvr.com`
- **Voiceover/caption:** “Solaris. The solar system, yours to explore. Visit solarisvr.com.”
- **Motion:** A final sun glow holds behind the wordmark. The URL appears last in solar orange for a clear end card.

## Production notes

- All visuals are self-generated React/SVG/CSS-style graphics. No external imagery, logos, music, or fonts are bundled.
- Copy is based on the public Solaris site at `https://solarisvr.com/` and `/spaceedu/` (product name, WebXR, browser launch, orbital data, NASA textures, and live Earth clouds).
- The existing repository uses Exo 2, Rajdhani, and Share Tech Mono. This isolated project uses system fallbacks to remain renderable offline; the letterforms and tracking emulate that treatment without downloading font assets.
