Planet

Uranus

The tipped-over ice giant whose poles take turns facing the Sun.

Fly to Uranus in 3D Hear it in a narrated tour

Overview

An ice giant that rotates on its side — its axial tilt is nearly 98°, possibly due to a massive ancient collision. Uranus has faint rings and a cold, pale blue-green atmosphere.

Three things to tell a class

  • Its 97.8° tilt means each pole gets 42 years of daylight followed by 42 of night.
  • Methane in the atmosphere absorbs red light, giving Uranus its pale cyan colour.
  • Only one spacecraft, Voyager 2, has ever visited, in 1986.
