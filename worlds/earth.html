Overview

Our home planet — the only known world harboring life. Earth has liquid water oceans, a protective magnetic field, and a just-right atmosphere for complex life to thrive.

Three things to tell a class

  • In the explorer, Earth’s cloud layer is refreshed daily from real satellite imagery.
  • The ISS ride-along shows the station’s real latitude, longitude, altitude and speed every five seconds.
  • Earth’s 23.4° axial tilt, not distance from the Sun, is what causes the seasons.
