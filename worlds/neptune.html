Neptune

The windiest planet and the first found by mathematics rather than by eye.

Overview

The windiest planet, with storms reaching 2,100 km/h. Neptune was the first planet predicted by mathematics before it was directly observed.

Three things to tell a class

  • Winds reach 2,100 km/h, faster than the speed of sound on Earth.
  • Neptune has completed only one full orbit since its discovery in 1846.
  • Its moon Triton orbits backwards and is probably a captured Kuiper Belt object.
