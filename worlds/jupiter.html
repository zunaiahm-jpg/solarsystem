Overview

The largest planet in the Solar System — a gas giant so massive that all other planets could fit inside it twice over. Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm that has raged for centuries.

Three things to tell a class

  • The Great Red Spot is a storm wider than Earth that has raged for at least 190 years.
  • Jupiter’s day is under ten hours, the shortest of any planet.
  • Its four largest moons, the Galileans, are visible with binoculars.
