Overview

Earth's only natural satellite, the Moon is the fifth-largest moon in the Solar System. It stabilizes Earth's axial tilt and drives our tides.

At a glance

Radius
1,737 km
Distance from Earth

Orbital Period

Surface Temperature

Three things to tell a class

  • The Moon is drifting away from Earth by about 3.8 cm each year.
  • It is tidally locked: the same face always points at Earth.
  • Twelve people have walked on it, all between 1969 and 1972.
