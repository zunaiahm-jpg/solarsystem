Mars

A cold desert that once had rivers, lakes and perhaps an ocean.

Overview

The Red Planet, named for its rust-colored surface. Mars hosts Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the Solar System, and Valles Marineris, the largest canyon system.

Three things to tell a class

  • Olympus Mons is about 22 km high, nearly two and a half times the height of Everest.
  • A Martian day (a sol) is only 39 minutes longer than an Earth day.
  • Rovers currently on the surface include Curiosity and Perseverance.
