Venus

Earth’s twin in size, its opposite in almost everything else.

Overview

The hottest planet in the Solar System due to a runaway greenhouse effect. Venus rotates backwards and has crushing atmospheric pressure 92 times that of Earth.

Three things to tell a class

  • Venus rotates backwards and so slowly that its day is longer than its year.
  • Surface pressure is 92 times Earth’s, about the same as 900 m under the ocean.
  • Clouds of sulfuric acid reflect 75% of sunlight, making Venus the brightest planet in our sky.
