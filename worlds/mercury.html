Mercury

Smallest planet, fastest orbit, wildest temperature swings.

Overview

The smallest planet in the Solar System and closest to the Sun. Mercury has no atmosphere, leading to extreme temperature swings from -180°C at night to 430°C during the day.

Three things to tell a class

  • A single Mercury day (sunrise to sunrise) lasts 176 Earth days: two full orbits.
  • Despite being closest to the Sun, Mercury is not the hottest planet. Venus is.
  • MESSENGER mapped the entire surface between 2011 and 2015.
